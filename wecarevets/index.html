More than a job board. A comprehensive pathway.

Transitioning from military to civilian life presents unique challenges that require more than just employment. We are designing a holistic model to support veterans and their families.

The WeCareVets initiative is being developed to bridge the gap between military service and civilian success by addressing careers, benefits, wellness, and community connection in a single, coordinated effort.

For Veterans

Meaningful careers and support tailored to your experience.

For Families

Resources and community for military spouses and children.

Experience gained in service deserves a meaningful path forward.

In Development

Planned Support Areas

Everyone's next mission looks different. We are building a flexible network of resources to address varied needs.

Careers & Skills

Planned connections to veteran-friendly employers, skills translation, training programs, and flexible work opportunities.

Benefits Navigation

Future tools to help untangle red tape and connect veterans with the benefits and services they have earned.

Health & Wellness

A planned directory and connection model linking veterans to physical and mental health resources in their local area.

Family Experiences

Envisioned programs focused on military spouses and children, recognizing that the whole family serves.

Community & Mentorship

The core of our planned initiative: building strong, supportive local networks. Connecting transitioning veterans with those who have walked the path before them to share advice, open doors, and provide a sense of belonging.

The Planned Connection Model

1. Discover Support

Assess needs, skills, and goals to find relevant opportunities and resources.

2. Connect

Link directly with employers, mentors, and local support organizations.

3. Take Next Step

Secure employment, access training, or engage with the community.
