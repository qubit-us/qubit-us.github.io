Why commute together?

Transforming the daily drive from a solitary routine into a shared opportunity for connection and efficiency.

Private Circles

Connect with approved members of your workplace circle. Commute with people you already share a professional community with.

Fewer Cars

Reduce parking congestion at the office and lower your collective carbon footprint by sharing rides that are already happening.

Scheduled Ease

Plan your week in advance. Coordinate regular routes or find a one-off ride when you need to head into the office.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rydes available to the public?

Not currently. Rydes is an upcoming product that will launch initially through private workplace pilots. You must have a verified work email from a participating company to join a circle.

Who can join a circle?

The initial pilot is designed for invited, approved workplace members with verified work email addresses. Workplace membership does not certify driving credentials.

When will the app launch?

A launch date has not been announced. Rydes is planned to begin with a private workplace pilot; this page previews the intended experience.
