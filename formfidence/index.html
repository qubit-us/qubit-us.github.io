Clear answers
about the concept.

The boundaries are part of the product.

Can I download Formfidence today?

Formfidence is in development. These screens explain the concept. Release timing, platform availability, supported forms, and pricing have not been announced.

Does it provide legal advice or decide eligibility?

The intended scope is document organization, preparation assistance, and identifying questions for review. It is not a substitute for qualified legal advice or an accredited representative, and it does not determine eligibility, medical causation, or benefit amounts.

Will my documents be sent to an online AI?

The proposed Private workspace processes documents and answers locally. Connect supplies public knowledge packages. Optional general research requires a separate preview and approval; no private document upload is part of that proposed flow. These boundaries still need implementation and security verification.

How will offline guidance stay current?

Versioned public packages would carry source references, retrieval dates, and supported-edition coverage. The workspace should display its freshness limits. Changed questions and checking rules require validation; downloading a new PDF alone does not make the system ready for that edition.

Is this affiliated with USCIS, VA, or Social Security?

No. Formfidence is a Qubit product concept, independent of government agencies. Applications and benefits decisions remain with the relevant authorities.

What is the accessibility goal?

We are designing toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Automated and keyboard checks help improve the concept screens, but full conformance is not established. The functioning product will need complete workflow and assistive-technology testing.
